### Two assist-mode refinements made while building (both in `06` §2 and M8-1)

1. **A story is forming only while its state can move on**: one of its exits has no gate, or a gate that
   holds now.
   - Without this, `door_lure` (activated at dawn, waiting in `home` for a lure) put a certain marker on
     Marti all day.
   - The same went for `roadworks_day.not_started` and `keeper_hours.closed_morning`.
2. **"Not yet watched" means some state the story's current state leads to has not been watched in this
   timeline.**
   - The plan said `frontier.storylets`, but Marti perceives every storylet he holds a role in from its
     first state. `door_lure` joins that list at dawn, so it could never have been marked.
3. **"Tied to a paid event" changed from my plan.** The plan read it as "has a charge payout", but 15 of
   Chapter 1's 16 storylets have one, so that marked nearly everything. Instead, a story is tied when a
   pinned card or power could advance it in its current state (a story with a card on it).
   - With `tea_break` on the roadworks, `roadworks_day` becomes markable.

## Deviations and notes

- **`?dev=1` stays** as the debug mode (M8-1 b), against the brief's "delete `?dev=1`".
- **What a tuning change re-runs:** the day from tick 0 to the frontier limit, with the player as it is.
  The renderer keeps the content it was made with, so `render.*` and `assist.cycleTicks` take effect
  on reload.
- **Loading a pin set on the normal page replaces the saved game;** the tab says so. The debug mode saves
  nothing.
- **Telemetry in the debug mode:** nothing is recorded, so the buffer holds only real play.
- **`recompute.ms`** is the time from the request to the first streamed frames, the quantity of
  `07` §4's budget. An event is recorded every 10th re-run and for every one over 100 ms.
- **`scroll_back`** is one backward move of 30 ticks or more: a drag, the shuttle back, or ←. It ends
  when the playhead moves forward again or the player acts.
- **The debug panel's text is 12 px** and outside the accessibility checks: it is developer-only.
- **Assist on Chapter 1, seed 4, untouched:** few markers. Most tied stories wait for something:
  - Rokko's walk waits for the rain or the boat;
  - the lure's story waits for a lure;
  - the crew's chatter forms only around 09:05.

  Pinned cards add the stories they could advance.
- **GitHub Pages:** the job deploys from `main` after the checks pass. Nothing has been merged to
  `main`, so nothing is deployed yet.
- **itch.io:** `.github/workflows/itch.yml` is a manual workflow. It needs the secret `BUTLER_API_KEY`
  and the variable `ITCH_TARGET` (e.g. `user/game:html5`). It has not been run.
