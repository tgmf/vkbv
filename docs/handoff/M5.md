## Speed

Playwright's headless build.

**`npm run bench`** (knobs 1000/3; budgets 100 ms, 120 ticks/s, observe 5 ms):

| Measure | M5 | M4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First streamed frames, median, per pin set | 65–79 ms | 71–115 ms |

| Observe at the edge: median / mean / 90th pct / max | 2.9 / 13.1 / 58.2 / 80–101 ms | 2.8 / 13.1 / 60.3 / 86–108 ms |

Runs vary about 10%, so M5 is no slower than M4. The budget is still short (M2-1, open).

**Samples** (brief item 8). Measured with a scratch script: a `road_both` re-run from 05:40 to 12:00,
seeds 49598663, 3762896133 and 3754479982.
- The per-tick traces take 29–37 ms of a 3.4–4.8 s re-run (0.8–0.9%).
- Drawing the 8 trajectories (`samplesOf`) takes 20–22 ms, once, when the re-run ends.
- In live playback each tick carries them forward. The observe timings above include that, and they
  equal M4's.

**In the browser:**
- **Frame time while scrubbing with all 12 cats** (64 ticks/s back and forward, 10:40, 1280×720):
  327–329 frames in 5.5 s; median 16.7 ms, 95th percentile 16.8 ms, longest 16.8 ms. No long task.
- **Live playback holds 16 ticks/s** (`player.observeMaxTps`) past 06:40.
  - Playback waits for each observe, and every fifth tick also steps the fog (50–90 ms).
  - Without catching that time up on the cheap ticks, it ran at 11 ticks/s (`docs/decisions.md`, M5).
- **Size:** 329.4 kB gzipped JS, budget 400 kB. three.js is now in: 226.8 kB main, 102.1 kB worker.

## Deviations and notes

- **Smoothing reaches the screen through the samples only.**
  - Ghosts' opacities stay the fog's filtered odds. Smoothed frames would break `03` §18's "a pin at
    t leaves every frame before t unchanged".
  - The focus layer's trajectories are drawn backward from the edge, so they follow the smoothed
    odds and end where a placement put the cat.
  - Samples sit beside the frames (`RecomputeResult.samples`), not inside them. `03` §14 and §16 are
    updated.
- **The "waits at the last arrived tick" item is not exercised in a browser.** In M5 a re-run comes
  only from loading a pin set or seed. That is a new game, whose frontier puts the edge at 0, so
  nothing streams for long. The rule (`forwardLimit`) has unit tests, and the controller applies it.
  M6's edits will give the browser case.
- **A drag is bounded by the playhead where it began.** `06` §3 allows no click or drag past the
  playhead, and the acceptance item says "anywhere up to the lantern edge". Both hold when the drag
  starts at the handle.
- **The dev page:**
  - its state panel updates only while paused (each request replays from a checkpoint, and during
    playback they starved the observes);
  - its text is developer-only, not translated, 12 px (as the M1.5 inspector was);
  - "reveal all" is on by default (brief item 6); off shows the map revealed as Marti sees it;
  - the seed is played as typed (M5-3); everywhere else the game uses the first seed of
    `vetted-seeds.json` (4).
- **Browser tests ignore one console message.** Chromium's GL driver logs "GPU stall due to
  ReadPixels" when Playwright screenshots the WebGL canvas. `smoke.spec.ts` and `viewer.spec.ts`
  filter exactly that text.
- **`tests/e2e/inspector.spec.ts` is replaced** by `viewer.spec.ts` (M5-2). Its checks live on:
  - Marti's place against a headless run (the five-tick test);
  - the lure's `pinFired` at 17:35 in the state panel's log.
- **`npm run check` ran twice in phase B.** The first run failed on one samples test: Vitest's 5 s
  default timeout under the full suite's load (1.3 s alone). It now has a 60 s limit, like the other
  heavy tests, and the second run passed. Phase A's commit includes a small change to frames' `lines`
  made while that phase's check ran; phase B's check covers it.
- **Graybox choices:**
  - place labels hide when zoomed far out (under 0.75);
  - bubbles over one cat stack, the last three only;
  - lines of a fogged cat anchor at its likeliest ghost;
  - the camera stays over the map;
  - rain is not drawn on places still under fog.
- **Things the renderer shows that are content behaviour, not bugs here:**
  - the crew's tale reaches Marti in his flat (M1-2);
  - the meeting happens with Marti at `beach.north` and Rokko under the boat.
