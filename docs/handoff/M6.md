## Deviations and notes

- **`empty_teapot` and seed 4 (M6-1).** Marti never voices `teaCraving` on the game's seed, so the card is
  never found. The road sets are played without its pin, and still reach the anchor on seed 4.
- **The 07:40 edit doesn't move the bar's end on seed 4** (above). The end's change is shown by withdrawing
  the lure.
- **A click where a valid cat stands over a valid place pins on the cat**, the hit drawn on top. The
  magnifying ring spreads such clusters, and a pin's label now names its target ("Tea Break on The
  roadworks at 08:40"), so the player can see what it went on. Keyboard focus aims exactly.
- **Holding an edit (M6-2).** While an edit is previewed or waits for the alert, the shuttle is paused and
  cards and powers can't be held. The timeline draws the edit's own pins meanwhile.
- **The presence strip is bright most of the day for a card that goes on any cat, over the roadworks.**
  Some cat is likely there from about 06:15 into the evening (the crew all day, others later). That is the
  content, not the strip (M6-4).
- **Browser test plumbing:**
  - the accessible target list carries screen positions (`data-x`, `data-y`); the playhead carries
    `data-pending`;
  - `interaction.spec.ts` logs the page's heap and DOM size beside each screenshot
    (`test-results/m6-memory.txt`). It stayed at 19–53 MB and 2–17 thousand nodes over the whole day;
  - an earlier run's "Target crashed" came while Playwright retried a hover on a pin under the
    playhead's handle for 5 minutes. The test now focuses the pin instead. The worker's host stayed at
    45 MB over repeated edits (measured in Node).
- **Out of the brief, not built:**
  - saving after every action (`04` §9; reloading is M7's);
  - the speculative preview while a card is held (an M2 handoff note, not in the spec);
  - hints, the teaser, settings, the debug panel.
- **Not committed:** an earlier `prettier --write src` rewrote line endings only in
  `src/sim/{effects,fog,pins,readers}.ts`, `src/content/schemas/{chapter,tuning}.ts` and
  `src/cli/calibration.ts`. They show as modified in `git status` with no content diff. My
  `git checkout` of them was denied, so they are left out of the commits; `git checkout -- <files>` clears
  them.
- **A spec problem I can't fix (yours):** `05` §5.2 has a sentence cut off at line 183
  (`(tests/golden/playback.s*.test.ts`,).
