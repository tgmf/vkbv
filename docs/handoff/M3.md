## Speed (`npm run bench`, Node 26, this machine)

| Measure | Value | Budget |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Re-run: first streamed frames (median, seeds 1–3) | 79–82 ms | 100 ms |
| Re-run rate | 99–103 ticks/s | 120 (fails below 40) |
| Observe at the edge, median over 05:40–09:40, seeds 1–3 | 2.4–2.8 ms | 5 ms (`budgets.observeMs`) |
| Observe at the edge, mean / 90th percentile / max | 11.6 ms / 52.7 ms / 81–140 ms | — |
| A whole day of live playback (seed 49598663, 1075 ticks) | 8.6 s (8 ms a tick) | — |

The median hides the shape: four ticks in five only step the lantern (2–3 ms); the fifth also steps the
fog (the cases, placement and evidence), 50–140 ms. At `player.observeMaxTps` (16 ticks/s, 62 ms a tick)
those ticks fit on average but the slowest stall a frame or two. The frames' new `visible`/`powers` cost
about 4% of the re-run rate (107 → 103 ticks/s in one batch). Runs vary about 10%.

The playthrough files take 14–31 s each (the pinned one vets seed 1 live, about 20 s); the whole unit
suite now takes 176 s (161 s before M3).
