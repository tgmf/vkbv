state (gates, scores, place options), building and sorting state keys in `normalise`, and
`starts` (place options, paths, duration splits): about 30 µs per state per step.

## Tagir's decisions of 2026-11-22: boredom, the road, settings, shuttle

**Principle kept:** in play the fog advances in lockstep with the lantern, one step per tick, with a
backward pass only at a collapse; nothing in play is computed past the lantern edge. Whole-day
projections are for seed vetting, calibration and strain only.

**1. Saturated top band (M2-7).** `fog.saturatedBandFrom` 950: n − 1 equal bands below it, one band
from it to 1 000, each read at its own midpoint (the top reads 975). Band moves walk each band's own
width. `fog.boredomWhole` stays, off. Golden files updated (decision-trigger keys use the bands).

**2. The road (M2-8), found at its source.** `scripts/crew-compare.ts`, 20 calibration seeds, crew
time and road counter, fog / reference:

| | foreman idle, 07:40 / 08:40 / 10:40 | crew working, same hours | road at 10:40 / 11:10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| before the fix | 66 / 55 / 39 % vs 85 / 100 / 95 % | 77 / 79 / 82 vs 74 / 75 / 76 % | 699 / 796 vs 688 / 764 |
| after the fix | 96 / 97 / 95 % vs 85 / 100 / 95 % | 72 / 73 / 73 vs 74 / 75 / 76 % | 648 / 733 vs 688 / 764 |

The foreman is the actor of the break choice, so he must not decide freely while the storylet is
`working`. The fog applied the storylet's `goTo @crew` to a fraction of his whole chain, so his
break-time work survived into `working`, where blocking then froze it. Role cats now carry the
storylet state that last directed them; effects move the source state's mass with the conditional
probability; blocking reads that state (decisions.md). Two earlier causes were fixed on the way:
counters truncated to whole units (now millionths), and boredom read at band midpoints that never
reach the cap (now the saturated band). The remaining shortfall (about 5% of work) is the step
granularity: a storylet transition reaches the crew at fog-step resolution.
