M1  A `blocked` edge counts as the activity ending: the cat decides again in phase 6 of the same tick (with the edge now known closed).
M1  A decaying mod gets `left = ceil(|add| / decayPerTick)`; `attracted` ends when its attract effect is gone; running effects count down in phase 1 including those created that tick.
M1  The start activity at tick 0 is chosen while building the initial state; its `activity` event is not part of any tick.
M1  The 03 §18 sweep over Chapter 1 (seeds 1–50 × streams 0–31) is split into eight test files by stream so Vitest runs them in parallel; `tests/invariants/check.ts` steps without keeping every state.

M2  `nearCat` in the fog reads each other cat's most likely state, computed once per case and step. M2  Band arithmetic: span = 1001 − floor, band(v) = trunc((v − floor) × bands / span), midpoint = floor + trunc((2b + 1) × span / (2 × bands)), width = trunc(span / bands). A move of more than one band moves whole bands, then the fraction.

With a lantern the pins stay with the lantern's `step` until M2 step 6 splits them between lantern and fog. A step's pins fire at its start (before the chains move, like phase 1), in log order; their effects are born at the pin's tick.
M2  Pin validity per case: a card on a cat, a place card and a power are valid or not whatever the state; an object card is valid with P(visible). At its first step `fizzled = 1e6 − v`; every step `fired += (1e6 − fired) × v`, and the primitives act with that new share. Pins before the fog's first tick are the lantern's and are skipped.
M2  A card's `delay` in the fog is its own chain-state field `forced` (fog steps left), not `interrupt` as the comment in `03` §13.2 says: a delayed cat stands and performs (its `while` effects, boredom, a storylet choice's actor), an interrupted one does nothing, and one field cannot hold both at once. A delay on a walking state stands it at the end of its current edge. A live cat's `forcedLeft` becomes `forced` in its point mass.
M2  Fog running effects (`Quantities.effects`) carry P(they exist); one is in a step when `born < t + K` and `t < born + left − 1` (phase 1 counts it down from its first tick). An offer born this step marks the mass it reaches as changed; `attracted` ends when no attract effect is at its place.
M2  Temporary mods in the fog (`Quantities.mods`, per case, so splits and merges carry them): a decaying card mod shifts back over `ceil(ceil(|add| / decayPerTick) / K)` steps by `trunc(left / leftSteps)` each, the rest at its last step; a held mod (`holdWhileRunning`, a storylet's `holdTicks`) all at once when it ends; both on the same share of the chain they moved.
M2  socialSpread in the fog: a cat's newly heard share of an event with hops left is re-emitted at the next step from where its chain stands, with one hop less (`Quantities.echoes`).
M2  Contributor heaps (`03` §13.5) keyed `fact:`, `flag:`, `heard:<cat>:`; a source is `sl:<storylet>.<state>` (its roles' cats), `act:<activity>` (the cat), `pin:<id>`, `ev:<event>` (the emitter; for `factIfPerceivedBy` also the perceiver) or `det:<id>` (its estimate, kept as a maximum). M2  `fogLeaf` reads `power` and `pin` leaves (`{ power, near: cat, m }`) as one cat's distance to the effect, not as the two-reference `near` condition.
M2  The backward pass lives in `src/sim/backward.ts` (a file not in `02` §2's list; `fog.ts` was already over 2 000 lines). Case weights are rescaled exactly (`floor(a × WAVE_TOTAL / total)` in two parts, so products stay below 2^53), the remainder to the first case, zero cases dropped. Smoothing waits for frame requests (step 9): nothing keeps earlier fog steps yet.
M2  `projectToEnd` steps a projection to the chapter's end (no lantern) and returns the raw fog, the anchor-conditioned one, the raw P(anchor) (strain, on a projection from the edge) and the fray (docs/questions.md M2-12). M2  The lantern (`src/sim/lantern.ts`): `advance` = the lantern's `step` (reading the fog through `lanternReader`, the case mixture by weight), the fog step at each boundary (reading the lantern and perceiving its events), then the edge (release at boundaries, placement or negative evidence for each fogged cat, held storylets going live once all their roles are live). The lantern state adds `unseen` (ticks since a protagonist last saw each live cat) for release. Fog events drawn into the lantern wait for Gaze and imposed facts.
M2  In the lantern's step (`ctx.live` set) a condition reads what the lantern holds — its facts, flags, the storylets it runs, its share of counters — from its own state as it changes during the step; the reader gives only the fog's part (`fogLeaf`, `flagHolds`). The fog, stepping after the lantern's ticks, reads the lantern's state at that point (`caseCtx`).
M2  Pins with a lantern: a card on a fogged cat is the fog's (the lantern skips it); a card on a live cat is the lantern's; place, object and power pins are fired by the lantern, and the fog then applies the parts that act on fogged cats and held storylets (offers come from the lantern's running effects). A lantern-run storylet's `trigger` of a held storylet is not passed to the fog (no Chapter 1 storylet does it).
M2  A storylet with a `nearCat` role is held by the fog while any cat is fogged (it could bind any of them); it goes live only once bound and all its roles are live.
M2  Fogged cats walk as live ones: each edge costs its ticks plus one (the edge starts on the tick after the cat stands at its first node, `03` §6 phase 3e); stand-ins interpolate the same way. Found by the exactness test; it moves default fog results (calibration in the handoff).
M2  Fog key changes trigger only on a step that holds a decision tick (`03` §8.2): mass whose decision key changed (`k`) or that carries `due` (a change seen between decision ticks) decides then; arrival, handed-back control, offers and an ended delay decide at once (`d`). With `fog.stepTicks` = the decision interval (5) every step is a decision step and nothing changes.
M2  Fog events reach the lantern (`03` §13.4, §15.1): each case keeps its step's own events (`q.stepEvents`, tagged with the storylet state that emitted them). A drawn one is case evidence and, unless it is a storylet's state event, an event of the lantern's next tick (perceived in phase 7); the rest is negative evidence. Who emitted it stays open.
M2  A fog-held storylet (or a lantern storylet the fog set going, e.g. by `trigger`) whose event a protagonist drew runs live from the state that emitted it, its other roles staying in the fog: the lantern's effects on fogged cats (`ctx.fogFx`) are applied to their chains at the next fog step; an emit from a fogged cat uses its most likely position.
M2  Where a detector runs follows the cats its clauses name (`namedCats`): with a lantern, one that names a fogged cat is the fog's, one that names none (weather, events, facts) the lantern's, which holds the world concretely; projections estimate both. (`detectorCats` keeps "no cat named = everyone" for perception.) Needed for Chapter 1's `boat_sails` (120 dry ticks after the rain), which the fog's estimate would collapse to the rain's stop.
M2  Links (Tagir, 2026-11-23): facts only storylets binding the followed protagonist set are lantern facts, never fog links; the first link slots go to counter races, one per counter (a fact set entering a state through an auto exit gated on that counter); at a race split each case's counter is conditioned on its side of the threshold with truncated-normal moments (the PHI table and its differences, integers only; a side under 0.1% pins the counter at the threshold).
M2  Calibration's reality draws its weather as the lantern does (`s0:branch` for the weather's branch points), so the run's odds and the reality share the world the run made concrete (07 §3).
M2  Watched facts (`03` §15.2): the summary also carries `self` (P's node, activity, interrupt) and `cats` (each cat P sees: node and activity), the parts a re-run compares. The fact of tick t is read after the step from t and its edge (`fact.tick = sim.tick − 1`), from that step's own events (`wave.tickEvents`); `digest` is `hash32` of the canonical JSON of `{tick, cat, summary}`. P perceives a storylet state it holds a role in or whose role cat it sees, a detector naming it, a cat it sees or no cat, and emitted events by `03` §4.
M2  Imposed facts (`03` §15.5) are compared at the edge of the step from their tick, before placement. A fogged cat a fact saw is forced only where the ordinary draws (`place:`/`pick:`) would not show it as watched, so an unedited re-run with its own watched facts is the same run; the forced state is drawn among the states with the watched node and activity, by mass (`obs`, `impose:<P>:<cat>`). The forced parts' probability for `reopenBelowPpm` is the product of their masses.
M2  Gaze (`03` §15.4) runs at the lantern edge after placement, so a cat a protagonist placed that tick is not gazed; the state a hit fixes is drawn among the circle's states by mass with purpose `gazepick:<pin>:<t>:<cat>` (the spec names only the first draw).
M2  Frames (`03` §16) are plain objects, one per tick (`Frame` in `types.ts`): per cat its ghosts `{ node, pos, activity, interrupt, p }` (a live cat one at 1 000 000; a fogged cat's grouped from its states over the cases, most likely first, the truncation remainder to the first, kept from the tick before when its chains and the case weights did not change); `happens` by emitted event name, `storylet:<id>.<state>` and `detector:<id>`; `rain`; `requirements` per top-level requirement item and `progress`. A frame's events are those of the step from its tick (as a watched fact reads them); a fog step's events go to the tick whose advance computed it. Compact arrays and transfers wait until the worker measures patch sizes (item 10).
M2  The re-run (`recompute.ts`) is a generator: it yields each tick's frame as it advances and returns the `Timeline` (request, checkpoints, edge wave, result), so the worker can batch patches and cancel a speculative preview. Each re-run gets a fresh decision cache (`03` §8.2: cleared when pins change).
M2  The edit tick is the earliest tick of a pin or watched fact that differs from the timeline replaced (0 for none or another seed; that timeline's edge when nothing differs). The re-run starts at the last checkpoint strictly before it, so the tick before the edit is replayed and can become the edge.
M2  Frontier limit (`04` §6, literally): tick t passes when its watched fact (the step from t) shows no main storylet outside `frontier.storylets`, `progress(t) ≤ story` and `t − reach ≤ pastTicks` (`reach` from tick 0, earlier ticks read from the kept frames); the edge is the tick before the first that fails (0 at tick 0). The last tick (fray, anchor's end, chapter's end) has no step, so only progress and pastTicks are checked there.
M2  Re-run ends: `fray` at the lantern's fray tick (`wave.fray`; for `fraysIf` that is the tick of the step that set `deadAt`, one before the wave that holds it); `anchor` (tick `reachedAt`) runs `end.afterAnchorTicks` past it; `chapterEnd` only if the chapter's last tick comes with neither (the lantern frays there without the anchor, so in practice never); `limit` otherwise. `breaks` are the reopened facts with a non-empty `paidBy`; Gaze facts are not part of the request, so none of them break here.
M2  `stats` carry per tick cases, live cats and fog states, plus `localCauses` and strain; the re-run time is the worker's (`src/sim` reads no clock). Strain is computed only when the request asks (the debug panel is open): `projectFrom` puts every live cat in the fog as a point mass and fixes the lantern's facts, flags, counters and storylet states in every case, then projects to the end; the lantern's running effects are not carried over (an approximation).
M2  `vetting.fog` (live vetting of a typed or bookmarked seed) starts at minStatePpm 20 000 and 2 bands, the coarse measuring knobs: `05` §4 names the knob without numbers.
M2  `checkSeed` stops running solutions once the seed passes (later runs cannot undo a pass), so a passing verdict's `storylets` counts the runs made so far. `npm run vet` checks candidates 1, 2, 3, … with the default knobs and writes the first N passing seeds to `src/content/data/ch1/vetted-seeds.json`; loading that list for a new game comes with the player and UI milestones.
M2  The worker is a thin wrapper around `src/bridge/host.ts` (`createHost(files, { post, schedule, now })`), which Vitest runs headless in Node; scripts and `src/cli` call `src/sim` directly (`02` §2 lets them import no `src/bridge`).
M2  The host keeps one current (committed) timeline and the last finished preview. Every `recompute` re-runs against the current timeline; `preview: false` replaces it, `preview: true` keeps the result apart; a new `recompute` discards the old preview, so only the last finished one can be committed. `commit` replies `ready` (`02` §3 gives it no reply of its own), or `error` without a preview.
M2  Streaming: the host runs a re-run in slices of `waves.streamEveryMs` (50), posts the frames of each slice as a `rerun` patch `{ from, frames }` (the first `from` is the replayed checkpoint; frames before it are the current wave's) and yields; a newer `recompute` drops the running one, which posts nothing more. The worker yields through a `MessageChannel`, not a timer (the worker runs no clock, `02` §3), so messages that arrived meanwhile go first.
M2  Frames cross the thread boundary as plain objects (structured clone), no compact arrays: on Chapter 1 a 50 ms patch is about 40 kB of JSON, a result to 12:00 (381 frames) about 2.7 MB, cloned in 5–7 ms in Node. The final `result` carries every frame (`03` §17's shape).
M2  `recompute` carries an optional `strain` flag (not in `02` §3): strain is a projection from the edge to the chapter's end, too slow for every edit, so only the debug panel asks for it. `stats.ms` is the re-run's time, measured by the host.
M2  `debugHistory` index 0 is the current timeline's lantern at the tick, replayed from its checkpoint, with the events of the step from it (none at the edge; ticks past the edge are an error); index i > 0 is `runHistory(c, seed, i, pins)` with the current pins, the last one cached. Both follow `History`'s convention: events at t happen going from t to t + 1.
M2  `npm run sim -- --fog` prints a whole-day run (`wholeDayRun` with a per-wave callback) at tick 0, each hour's first tick and the last; `--projection` the fog alone from tick 0 (`projectionFog` of the initial wave, `projectToEnd` with a per-step callback). Strain at each hour is a projection to the end each, minutes on Chapter 1, so it prints only with `--strain` (Tagir, 2026-11-24). The report is `src/cli/fog-report.ts`, a pure function the tests call; storylet odds come from `storyletChances` (frames.ts): a storylet the lantern runs is certain in its state, one the fog holds is its mixture.
M2  Calibration runs in parallel: `scripts/calibrate.ts` splits the (pin set, seed) runs over child processes (`--jobs`, default the CPU count; `fork` of the same script) and judges the collected pairs; one process and four give the same table. Its core (`seedPairs`, `judge`) is `src/cli/calibration.ts`. Without `--pinset` it runs both of `07` §3's sets.
M2  `fog.counterByChain` (on by default since 2026-11-25, `b081a50`; M2-8/M2-23): each chain entry carries `w`, what its mass has added to counters through activities' `while` effects (mean in thousandths, variance in units² × 1000); a step stamps every entry made from a source entry with its work plus this step's, chain transforms carry it, `normalise` and case merges mix it, and the case's counter moves by the change of each cat's work mixture instead of a Bernoulli term. Off, nothing carries `w` and every result is byte-identical (fog hashes checked).
M2  Merged fog cases take each cat's decision conditions (`conds`) from the heaviest part, not the first: a sliver merged in first (a race split's piece) made the case's mass decide on every decision step.
M2  With `fog.counterByChain`, a race counter's storylet (the first whose auto exit races on it) also carries work by state (`FogStorylet.w`): each step the work of mass tagged with a state (`sl`) is credited per unit of the storylet's mass in that state; the work moves with the storylet's transitions and mixes on case merges. The growth of its spread over the states is work the cats share (a break the crew takes together); the counter gains it less the part each cat's own carried work counts (Σ mᵢ² / (Σ mᵢ)², by mean work). A state is credited its average rate, so a short spell counts more than the walking cats do in it: in the tiny crew test the shared spread runs about 30% wide (before both carries the road's spread was about a third too narrow and the fog overconfident).
M2  A transition split's case where the transition happened drops the facts a once-storylet set from states that cannot lead to the state reached (a competing exit of the same step: `abandoned` beside `finished`); a once-storylet keeps its facts by source state, so they come out exactly. State link splits (`linkSplit`) are left as they are.
M2  Calibration pairs each event's forecast with the reality (`docs/questions.md` M2-26): its chance just before the protagonists' perception first moves it by `calibration.observedPpm` (10 000), else its chance at the end; `advance` and `wholeDayRun` take a `beforeEvidence` observer for it (it changes nothing). Events the lantern holds keep 1 000 000.

M2  The quick calibration (60 seeds) is not in `npm run check` (M2-23: it joins only once it passes; with test content it does not); run it by hand with `npm run calibrate -- --quick`.
M3  A timeline keeps each tick's watched facts (`views`, the followed cats' `watch` of every stepped tick) and the wave past the edge (`ahead`, already stepped when the limit stopped the run). `rerun` and `extendEdge` (live playback at the edge, `recompute.ts`) share one per-tick step (`tickOf`), so playback and a re-run over the same ticks are the same lantern (tested on the tiny world and Chapter 1). The host's `observe` reads `views` behind the edge and calls `extendEdge` at it; each observed fact joins the host timeline's `watched`, so the next edit's edit tick does not take playback facts for an edit.
M3  `observed` also carries the new edge, the timeline's end and the gaze facts at the new edge tick (`02` §3 updated): the UI must know when playback frays or the anchor ends it, and Gaze facts happen during live playback too.
M3  Frames carry `visible` (P(visible) of objects with `visibleWhen`) and `powers` (powers available at the tick), evaluated with the lantern's fog reader (`03` §16 updated): `04` §3 and §8 judge them "in the frame at the playhead". It costs about 4% of the re-run rate (107 → 103 ticks/s, same batch).
M3  `watch` names the followed cat's own voice `voice:<hook>` and another cat's, heard, `voice:<cat>:<hook>`, so `04` §7's "the followed cat's voice" can be read from the summary.
M3  Player actions return a typed `Refusal`; pins get ids `pin<seq>`; a card's `pin` field is set only in zone `pinned`.
